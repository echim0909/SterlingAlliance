OUR FOCUS

Building projects with the right partners.

We welcome conversations with claim holders, operators, investors and service providers. Our areas of interest include developing joint ventures, mobilising suitable equipment, identifying and pegging claims, and connecting projects with appropriate technical and legal expertise. Each opportunity is considered on its merits, with due regard for Zimbabwean law, permitting and responsible practice.
